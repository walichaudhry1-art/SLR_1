Incident Summary

At 19:12:05Z, ar-updates@freight-carrier-portal.com delivered phishing emails with Carrier_Statement_Nov2026.iso to c.brennan and p.linn. Only c.brennan mounted and executed the payload chain on WS-AP-011: explorer.exe launched wscript.exe running Invoice_Verify.vbs at 19:15:40Z, which spawned powershell.exe -NoP -W Hidden -enc ... (PID 7204) at 19:16:22Z. Script block logging (Event ID 4104) showed PowerShell downloading coldroute_svc.exe from http://coldchain-billing-sync.com/srv/coldroute_svc.exe (192.0.2.77) into C:\Users\c.brennan\AppData\Local\Temp\coldroute_svc.exe and launching it (PID 7410). Persistence was added via HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run\ColdRouteAgent at 19:17:02Z, followed by TCP/443 C2 beacons to 192.0.2.77 and a failed SMB logon (Event ID 4625, 0xC0000022) to FS-COLD-01 (10.50.10.12) at 19:21:18Z.

Recommended Response

  • Isolate WS-AP-011 via EDR, reset CHESAPEAKE\c.brennan credentials/MFA, and purge the phishing message from mailboxes.
  • Block coldchain-billing-sync.com and 192.0.2.77; delete Run key ColdRouteAgent and quarantine coldroute_svc.exe.
  • Escalate to Tier 2 IR to validate FS-COLD-01 and check for any other hosts mounting Carrier_Statement_Nov2026.iso.
